# Training & Employee License Validator — Business Rules

1. Employee ID must not be blank and must exist in Employee Master.
2. Employee name and department must match the Employee Master.
3. Training Code must exist in Training Master.
4. Training Name and Operation must match the Training Master.
5. Training Status must be Completed before a license can be issued.
6. Assessment Result must be Pass before a license can be issued.
7. Training Completion Date and License Issue Date must be valid dates and cannot be in the future.
8. License Issue Date cannot precede Training Completion Date.
9. License Expiry Date must match the approved validity period in Training Master.
10. A license expires on its calculated expiry date; on or after that date, it is expired.
11. Licenses expiring within the next 30 days must be flagged for renewal.
12. Duplicate Employee ID + Training Code records must be identified.
13. Mandatory fields must be populated.
14. Inconsistent training statuses and assessment results must be flagged.
15. An employee is eligible only when all critical validation rules pass and the license is currently valid.
16. Invalid or expired records must not be included in the upload-ready file.
17. Valid licenses approaching expiry may remain eligible until their expiry date, but must carry a renewal warning.
18. Only approved employee-training-operation mappings may be included in the upload file.
19. Validation issues must be recorded for HR review rather than silently discarded.
## Manager Approval Rules

1. Manager approval status must be Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
2. Only an authorized manager may approve an employee's operation license.
3. Manager name and approval date are mandatory when approval status is Approved or Rejected.
4. Approval date must be a valid date and cannot be in the future.
5. An employee with Pending approval is not eligible to perform the operation.
6. An employee with Rejected approval is not eligible to perform the operation.
7. Manager approval cannot override incomplete training, failed assessment, invalid records, or an expired license.
8. Only records passing the required validation rules and having Approved manager status can be included in the upload-ready file.
9. Approval and rejection decisions must be included in the employee license status report.
10. Rejected records must include a manager remark explaining the decision.


Assumption: License expiry is calculated as one calendar year after issue, minus one day. For example, a license issued on 2026-05-10 expires on 2027-05-09. The organization must confirm this convention before production use.
